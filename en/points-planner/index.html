Questions people ask

What can I book with 75,000 Chase points?

One way in business class to Europe with room to spare, like Iberia nonstop to Madrid for 40,500 Avios, or one way to Tokyo on ANA through Aeroplan. Chase points transfer to Aeroplan, Flying Blue, British Airways, Iberia, Singapore, United and Virgin Atlantic.

What can I book with 75,000 Capital One miles?

One way in business class to Madrid on Iberia, to South America on Avianca or Copa through LifeMiles, or to Asia through Aeroplan. Capital One miles also transfer to Flying Blue, Turkish, Qatar and more.

What can I book with 100,000 Amex points?

A round trip in business class to Madrid on Iberia for 81,000 Avios, or one way to Asia or South America with points left over. Amex transfers to Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles, Delta, Flying Blue, British Airways, Iberia, Qatar and more.

What are Rove miles?

Rove is a free program that earns airline miles when you shop, dine or book hotels through it, no credit card needed. New members get 1,500 miles at signup, and miles transfer to programs like Aeroplan, Flying Blue, Virgin Atlantic, Qatar and Lufthansa Miles & More, which no credit card transfers to. You need a US phone number to sign up.

Will these exact flights still be available?

Probably not those exact dates. Award space changes daily. The value of this page is showing what your points can realistically do, so you pick the right card and program before you start.
