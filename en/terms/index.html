Award booking service

The fee is $250 per person, one way or roundtrip. A $100 per person deposit starts the search; it is nonrefundable and counts toward the fee. The $150 balance is charged only when your flights are booked. We cannot make an airline release award seats and cannot guarantee them; good seats can disappear in minutes, so when an option fits your request we move fast. If nothing bookable opens within 90 days of daily checks, the search ends and the deposit is all you pay; if your trip is further out, we agree on when your 90 days start, usually closer to the trip. If we find flights that match what you asked for and you decide not to book them, the full fee is due. Your points pay for the flights, and airline taxes and fees are charged to your card at booking. We will never ask for your passwords; your points stay in your accounts, and transfers happen only after you have seen and approved the exact flights, because transfers cannot be undone.
