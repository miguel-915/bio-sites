Flight deal FAQ

Where do these flight deals come from?

I subscribe to Find Flights for Me, Going and Thrifty Traveler Premium. Each service has a team that watches airfare and sends alerts when prices drop well below normal. I share a preview of their best deals here every week.

Why don't you list the departure cities and dates?

The full details belong to each service's members. Join the one that found the deal to get the departure airports, exact dates and booking links.

Are these prices roundtrip?

Cash fares are roundtrip in US dollars unless a deal says "each way." Points deals show the miles or points needed and are usually priced each way.

How fast do flight deals disappear?

Many sales last only a few hours to a few days. If a deal fits your plans, check it quickly and book before the fare goes back up.

Which flight deal service should I use?

Find Flights for Me focuses on business class deals on cash and points. Going sends cash fares from the airports you choose. Thrifty Traveler Premium covers cash fares plus points and miles deals. Straight to the Points texts you award flight deals the moment they open up.
