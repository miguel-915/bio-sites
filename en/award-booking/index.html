Award booking FAQ

Is the deposit refundable?

No. The $100 deposit covers the time we spend searching for your trip, whether or not seats open up.

Can you guarantee seats?

No one can. Award space changes every day. Flexible dates, nearby airports and an open mind on airlines give you the best odds.

Should I transfer my points now?

No. Don't move any points until we confirm a seat with you. Transfers to airlines can't be undone.

What points work?

Bank points like Amex, Chase, Capital One, Citi and Bilt are the most flexible. Airline miles you already have work too. Tell us everything you have.

Are taxes included?

No. Airline taxes and fees are paid by you when the ticket is booked and are not part of our fee.
