Award search tool FAQ

What's the best award search tool?

For most people, seats.aero. It covers the most programs and shows every date with space at once. point.me is better when your dates are fixed and you want booking instructions for your specific points.

What's the difference between award search tools and deal alerts?

Award search tools are for when you know where you want to go. Deal alerts come to you and work best when you're flexible about where you travel.

Do I need all of these?

No. One search tool and one deal alert service cover most trips. Add ExpertFlyer when you start watching specific flights.

Are these tools free?

Most have a free version with limits and a paid plan that unlocks full searches or every alert. Try the free version first and upgrade the one you use most.
