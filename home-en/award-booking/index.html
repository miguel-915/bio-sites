One honest note before we start

I cannot create award seats, and I cannot make an airline release them. Sometimes the dates you want simply do not open, especially in business and first class, around holidays, and on popular routes.

What I promise is effort: I will search hard, keep a continuous watch on your dates, and bring you the best option that actually exists at that moment.

And when I find something that fits, we move fast. Award seats can disappear in minutes, sometimes while we are looking at them. If you like an option I send you, say yes quickly so we can lock it in. Leaving it overnight usually means it is gone by morning.

Fixed dates are welcome

Award seats on exact dates do not always open right away. That is what the tracker is for: if the first options are not good enough, I keep watching your dates until the seats appear or your deadline passes.

Every tracker search runs for a 90 day tracking window. If your trip is further out than that, we agree on when your 90 days start, usually closer to the trip, so the tracking covers the weeks when seats are most likely to open.

If nothing bookable opens by the end of your search, it simply ends, and the deposit is all you ever paid. And if I find flights that match what you asked for and you decide not to book them, the full fee is due, because the work is done.

One thing I will never ask for

Your passwords, at any level. Your points stay in your accounts, and on the screen share you are the one who logs in. Points transfers cannot be undone, so we only make them once you have seen and approved the exact flights.

Is this a fit?

This is a fit if:

  • You already have the points, or close to them, sitting in your accounts
  • Your trip is at least a few weeks out, so the search has room to work
  • You can say yes fast when the right seats appear

It is probably not a fit if:

  • You do not have the points yet. Build them first; that is what the Bootcamp is for
  • You need a guaranteed seat on one exact date no matter what. Award space does not work that way, and anyone who promises it is guessing
  • Your trip leaves in the next few days. There is no runway for the tracker then, though I will still take a quick first look if you ask
