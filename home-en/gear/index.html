Travel gear FAQ

Can I bring a power bank on a plane?

Yes, in your carry-on. Airlines don't allow spare lithium batteries in checked bags, and most limit size to 100 watt-hours, which covers nearly every phone power bank.

Are packing cubes worth it?

Yes, especially for carry-on only trips or families. They keep things organized and compression cubes free up space.

Away or Béis: which luggage is better?

Both are durable hard-shell suitcases. Away is simple and sturdy; Béis has more pockets and compartments. Pick based on how you like to organize.

What's the best neck pillow for sleeping on a plane?

I use the trtl because it supports your head without the bulk of a U-shaped pillow and packs flat.
