Award sweet spot FAQ

What is an award sweet spot?

It's a redemption where a points program charges much less than the seat is worth, often because of a fixed award chart or a partner airline priced low. Business class to Madrid for 40,500 Avios instead of $4,000 cash is a classic example.

Which credit card points work best for sweet spots?

Flexible points from Amex, Chase, Capital One, Citi, Bilt and Wells Fargo transfer to the airline programs behind most sweet spots. Each row above lists which cards transfer to that program.

How often does this page update?

Every morning. Award seats come and go fast, so confirm the seat on the airline's site before you transfer points.

Do sweet spots last forever?

No. Programs change prices and partners without much notice, which is why it pays to use a good sweet spot while seats are open.
