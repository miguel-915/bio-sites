Questions

Before you join

When can I join?

Enrollment opens Friday, November 27, 2026. Founders, the first 25 seats at $297, go first. Join the waitlist and the checkout link lands in your inbox first.

I have no points and no travel cards. Is this for me?

Yes. The course starts at zero: how credit works, how to become card ready, and which card to open first, in what order.

What credit score do I need?

Card approvals need good credit, and no one can guarantee an approval. The course starts with a credit path so you know where you stand before you apply for anything.

What if I want help finding the flights?

That is what my award booking search is for, and it stays separate from the membership. It finds the seats and guides you through booking them in your own account, so your points and your accounts stay under your control. Inside the membership, the course, the calls, and FLIGHT WATCH teach you to run the same search yourself.

How does FLIGHT WATCH work?

You send one trip you want. For 90 days we watch it with award alerts, manual checks, and deal newsletters, and we check often. When a seat that fits opens, you hear about it and you book it. It is a watch, not a daily search service.

Can my spouse learn with me?

Yes. One membership covers your household. Both partners can watch the course and hold cards in their own names, so the house earns welcome bonuses twice as fast. The benefits, like the flight watch and the calls, count once per household. One membership. Two players. Double the welcome bonuses.

What if I do the work and still do not travel?

That is the guarantee. Finish the core modules, attend six group calls, start one FLIGHT WATCH request with bookable dates, and follow your card plan. If no trip is redeemed in 12 months, your next 12 months are free.

What happens after my 12 months?

The private community renews at $300 per year after your first 12 months. Refunds follow the published terms.
