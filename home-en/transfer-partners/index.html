Good to know
  • Bilt to Marriott is 1:1, plus a 5,000 point Marriott bonus for every 20,000 Bilt points moved in one transfer. Marriott points also move on to nearly 40 airlines at 3:1, with a 5,000 mile bonus for every 60,000 points transferred to most airlines, and 10,000 bonus miles to United. American, Delta and Avianca get no bonus.
  • Chase to Hyatt depends on your card. Since October 2026, Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred move at 4:3, so 1,000 points become 750. Sapphire Reserve cards still move at 1:1.
  • Citi ratios shown are for premium Citi cards such as Strata Elite and Strata Premier. Other Citi cards can move at lower ratios.
  • Amtrak Guest Rewards is a train program that rides along in the airline table.
  • Avios programs (Aer Lingus, British Airways, Iberia, Finnair and Qatar Airways) can move Avios between each other once your points land.
Transfer partner questions

Which credit cards transfer to American Airlines?

Citi ThankYou points are the only major bank currency that transfers directly to American Airlines AAdvantage, at 1:1 with premium Citi cards. Points from Amex, Bilt, Capital One, Chase, Rove and Wells Fargo can still book American Airlines flights through oneworld partner programs such as British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Iberia and Qatar Airways.

Which banks transfer to United Airlines?

Chase Ultimate Rewards and Bilt Points transfer to United MileagePlus at 1:1. United flights can also be booked with Star Alliance partner programs that banks reach, such as Air Canada Aeroplan, ANA Mileage Club and Lufthansa Miles and More.

Can Amex points book airlines that Amex does not transfer to directly?

Yes. American Express Membership Rewards reach one or more programs in every alliance, and each of those programs can book the rest of its alliance. Aeroplan books Star Alliance, Air France KLM Flying Blue books SkyTeam, and British Airways and Qatar Airways book oneworld. The What you can book section above shows every airline behind each program.

Which transfer partner is best for hotel stays?

World of Hyatt is the standout for value, reached from Bilt at 1:1 and from Chase at 4:3 on most cards, or 1:1 from Sapphire Reserve. The hotel table above compares all nine hotel transfer partners and every bank ratio into each one, including Marriott Bonvoy, Hilton Honors and IHG One Rewards.

Do transfer ratios and partners change?

Yes. Ratios and partner lists change, and limited time transfer bonuses come and go. Always confirm in your card portal before you transfer, because transfers cannot be undone.
