How to transfer Capital One miles

Capital One miles transfer to a solid list of airlines and hotels, right from the site or the app. The two details that trip people up are the name match, because the name on your Capital One account must match the loyalty account exactly, and the ratio, because Capital One does not move every partner at 1:1. Find the seat first, then transfer.

1. Confirm the award and open your rewards

Verify the flight or hotel night on the program that will book it. Then sign in to Capital One, open the card that earns your miles, and go to View Rewards. Choose the option to transfer or convert rewards to a travel partner. Moving miles to a partner is a different action from booking cash travel inside Capital One Travel.

2. Choose the partner and link the account

Pick the program that quoted your award and enter the loyalty account number. Capital One requires the name on your account to match the name on the loyalty account, so this is a transfer to your own accounts. Check the number digit by digit before you move on.

3. Enter the amount using the partner ratio

The published minimum is 1,000 miles. Use the ratio shown for your partner to work out the amount: most partners move at 1:1, and a few move lower, so the same balance can buy different amounts in different programs. Subtract any miles already waiting in the receiving account.

4. Review and submit

Recheck the program, the name, the number, the amount, and the ratio on the review screen, then submit. Transfers are final. Wait until the miles post inside the loyalty account, confirm the award is still there at the same price, and book it.

Capital One rules worth knowing

  • The name on your Capital One account must match the loyalty account name. Plan on transferring only to your own accounts.
  • The minimum transfer is 1,000 miles.
  • Ratios vary by partner. Most are 1:1, and a few are lower, so always read the ratio for the partner in front of you.
  • Many Capital One transfers post quickly, but the screen estimate governs your transfer. Book only after the miles land.
